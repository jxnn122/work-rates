PRIVACY NOTICE.

A clear summary of the anonymous information this site records and what happens when you follow an external contact link.

LAST UPDATED / 7 AUGUST 2026

Questions about your information can be sent by messaging @jxnn122 on X.

01

Anonymous website analytics

The site records anonymous page views and interactions such as showreel plays, project-link clicks and contact-button clicks. These records may include the page, broad device category and referring website. They do not contain names, direct-message contents, cookies or stored IP addresses.

02

Contacting JXNN on X

Contact buttons open the JXNN profile on X. Any message you send there is handled by X under its own terms and privacy policy; this website does not receive or store the contents of that message.

03

External links

Portfolio, social and contact links may open third-party services. Those services control the information they collect. Check their privacy settings before sharing footage, references or personal details.

04

Sensitive information

Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive personal information through social direct messages. Share project assets only through a secure link with appropriate access settings.
